# Interactive Gearmotor Section — Editorial Technical Experience

## Purpose
Create a premium interactive technical section for the CBL website that explains an industrial gearmotor as an engineering system rather than as a decorative 3D gimmick.

The experience should feel like an industrial design / engineering editorial feature, not an AI-generated landing-page component.

## Product Representation
Use an **unbranded industrial helical-bevel gearmotor** with believable mechanical geometry.

### Required visual characteristics
- industrial motor + gearbox assembly
- credible angular gearbox housing
- input shaft
- multi-stage reduction gearing
- bevel gear pair
- output shaft
- bearing set
- oil seal set
- motor / gearbox interface
- realistic bolts, covers, machining and mechanical interfaces
- material response consistent with cast metal, machined steel, bearings and rubber seals

### Prohibited representation
- no SEW wordmark
- no manufacturer logo
- no nameplate
- no serial number
- no QR code or barcode
- no fabricated manufacturer markings
- no fake certification labels
- no glowing sci-fi materials
- no exaggerated transparent neon internals

The model may be visually inspired by real industrial helical-bevel gearmotor proportions, but it must remain unbranded and should not claim to be an exact manufacturer model.

## Visual Material Direction
Preferred exterior treatment:
- neutral industrial gray housing
- medium gray motor body
- darker fasteners
- brushed / machined steel shaft surfaces
- dark rubber oil seals
- restrained roughness and realistic edge treatment

Avoid glossy showroom plastic, excessive orange, unrealistic chrome, and decorative glow.

CBL orange should be used sparingly as an interface accent only.

## 01 / Drive Motor
### Heading
**Where rotational power begins.**

### Copy
Motor menghasilkan putaran awal yang kemudian diteruskan ke gearbox untuk dikurangi kecepatannya dan ditingkatkan torsinya sesuai kebutuhan equipment.

### Technical note
`INPUT POWER → ROTATIONAL MOTION`

### Motion
- isolate motor visually
- move motor only slightly away from gearbox
- keep mechanical centerline understandable
- no glow

---

## 02 / Input Shaft
### Heading
**Transfers power into the gearbox.**

### Copy
Input shaft menghubungkan motor dengan tahap reduksi pertama. Alignment dan kondisi bearing pada area ini berpengaruh langsung terhadap kestabilan sistem.

### Technical note
`MOTOR → INPUT SHAFT → REDUCTION STAGE`

### Motion
- housing may become partially transparent
- retain shaft alignment
- rotational cue should be subtle and technical, not decorative

---

## 03 / Reduction Gearing
### Heading
**Lower speed. Higher usable torque.**

### Copy
Susunan gear mengurangi kecepatan motor melalui beberapa tahap sekaligus meningkatkan torsi sebelum tenaga diteruskan menuju output.

### Technical note
`HIGH SPEED INPUT → GEAR REDUCTION → HIGH TORQUE OUTPUT`

### Motion
This is the principal internal reveal. Gear relationships must remain spatially understandable. Do not explode parts far away from their real assembly position.

---

## 04 / Bevel Gear Set
### Heading
**Redirecting power through the drivetrain.**

### Copy
Bevel gear memungkinkan arah transmisi tenaga berubah di dalam gearbox sekaligus mempertahankan transfer torsi menuju tahap output.

### Technical note
`RIGHT-ANGLE POWER TRANSFER`

### Motion
- separate the gear pair only enough to clarify engagement
- preserve the relation between gear axes
- avoid dramatic floating-parts animation

---

## 05 / Bearing Set
### Heading
**Supporting every rotating component.**

### Copy
Bearing mempertahankan alignment shaft dan mengurangi friction selama operasi. Wear, contamination, atau lubrication yang tidak sesuai dapat menyebabkan vibration, noise, dan peningkatan temperatur.

### Technical note
`ALIGNMENT / LOAD / LUBRICATION`

### Motion
Focus on the bearing position relative to the shaft and housing. Use restrained contrast or outline rather than illumination effects.

---

## 06 / Oil Seal
### Heading
**Keeping lubricant in. Contamination out.**

### Copy
Oil seal membantu mempertahankan lubricant di dalam gearbox sekaligus membatasi masuknya debu, air, dan kontaminan dari lingkungan kerja.

### Technical note
`SEALING / CONTAMINATION CONTROL`

### Motion
- subtle focus / outline only
- keep seal seated close to its actual operating position

---

## 07 / Output Shaft
### Heading
**Where torque reaches the machine.**

### Copy
Setelah melewati tahap reduksi, output shaft meneruskan torsi menuju conveyor, pump, agitator, atau equipment lain yang digerakkan.

### Technical note
`REDUCED SPEED / INCREASED TORQUE`

### Motion
- begin reassembling the system visually
- make output side the focal point
- camera may shift slightly toward output axis

**More than an enclosure.**

Housing menjaga posisi gear, shaft, bearing, dan lubricant dalam satu struktur mekanis yang presisi sekaligus melindungi komponen internal selama operasi.

# Reassembly Transition
After the component sequence, return the gearmotor toward its assembled state.

### Transition copy
`COMPONENTS WORK AS A SYSTEM`

**Reliability depends on more than one individual part.**

The assembled unit may rotate approximately 10–15 degrees to restore spatial understanding. Keep the movement slow and restrained.

# Interaction Sequence
1. Intro / full assembly
2. Motor separates subtly
3. Housing transitions to ghost view
4. Input shaft focus
5. Reduction gearing reveal
6. Bevel gear focus
7. Bearing focus
8. Oil seal focus
9. Output shaft focus
10. Housing / system context
11. Reassembly
12. Technical support CTA

The scroll experience should teach the mechanical relationship between parts. Motion is explanatory, not decorative.

---

# Desktop Composition
Preferred composition is an editorial split layout:

- textual narrative approximately 35–42% width
- 3D model approximately 58–65% width
- generous negative space
- sticky/pinned model is acceptable only if scrolling remains predictable
- no glass panel behind text
- no floating card UI
- no ring of hotspot buttons surrounding the model

Illustrative structure:

```text
03 / REDUCTION GEARING

Lower speed.
Higher usable torque.                [ LARGE 3D GEARMOTOR ]

Supporting copy...

HIGH SPEED INPUT → REDUCTION → OUTPUT
```

---

# Mobile Composition
Do not merely stack the desktop layout mechanically.

Preferred order:
1. step index
2. heading
3. large 3D viewport
4. explanatory copy
5. technical microcopy

The 3D viewport may occupy roughly 50–60% of viewport height when useful. Preserve readable controls and avoid scroll trapping.

---

# Typography
- step index / eyebrow: 11–12px, uppercase, wide tracking
- primary heading: 48–72px desktop; 32–42px mobile
- body: 16–18px
- technical microcopy: 11–13px, mono or semi-mono treatment

Typography should act as interface. Do not wrap every text group in a card.

---

# Motion Rules
Preferred:
- masked text reveal
- opacity + small positional change
- subtle camera interpolation
- modest separation of mechanical components
- ghosted housing transparency
- 3D model scale changes only when spatially useful

Avoid:
- bounce
- elastic easing
- constant floating
- excessive parallax
- scroll hijacking
- fast rotations
- glowing active parts
- exploded parts travelling implausibly far
- every element fading upward at the same time

Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` with a stable readable fallback.

---

# Engineering Credibility Rules
- preserve correct mechanical relationships even in stylized presentation
- do not invent gear ratios, RPM, torque, dimensions or material specifications
- do not label the visual as a specific manufacturer model unless verified
- do not imply OEM authorization
- avoid claims that cannot be supported by CBL project records
- internal components should appear physically plausible, not generated as arbitrary gear shapes

---

# Definition of Done
The section is not complete until:
- desktop and mobile compositions both feel intentionally designed
- the model remains the visual focus without overpowering the content
- all eight component steps have distinct but consistent visual states
- motion explains mechanical structure
- the system is usable without motion
- no manufacturer identity appears on the model
- no AI-template patterns have been introduced
- screenshots are reviewed at 1440px, 1024px and 390px
- `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npm run lint`, and `npm run build` pass after implementation
