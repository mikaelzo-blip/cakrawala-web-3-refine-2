## Mandatory Design Direction
Before changing any visual layout, component styling, homepage section, animation, typography, or responsive composition, read and follow `DESIGN_DIRECTION.md`.

The design direction is intentionally human-led and anti-template. Do not generate a full generic website from scratch. Work section-by-section, preserve real company/project information, and avoid patterns explicitly prohibited in `DESIGN_DIRECTION.md`.

When redesigning, prioritize:
- editorial + industrial + architectural composition
- authentic CBL project photography
- typography and spacing over decorative cards
- asymmetric project storytelling
- restrained, purposeful motion
- mobile composition as a designed layout, not merely stacked desktop content
